---
title: Who Is RIA Hub For?
description: Learn which teams and workflows RIA Hub is designed for.
---

RIA Hub is designed for AI-powered radio and Software-Defined Radio (SDR) workflows.

<h2
  style={{
    color: 'light-dark(black, white)'
  }}
>
  Common Use Cases
</h2>

- organize and manage RF datasets
- train AI models for radio signals
- prototype SDR applications
- test signal-processing workflows
- deploy models to radio or edge environments
- collaborate on wireless AI experiments

<h2
  style={{
    color: 'light-dark(black, white)'
  }}
>
  Target Users
</h2>

- **SDR Engineers** — Build and optimize intelligent radio applications using integrated dataset, model, and deployment workflows.

- **Wireless AI Researchers** — Accelerate experimentation for RF machine learning, spectrum analysis, and signal intelligence research.

- **Telecom and OpenRAN Teams** — Train and deploy AI models for software-defined radio and next-generation wireless infrastructure.

- **Defense and Spectrum Operations** — Prototype spectrum-awareness and intelligent sensing applications for operational environments.

- **Universities and Research Labs** — Standardize collaborative RF AI experimentation with reusable workflows and datasets.

- **Embedded Systems Developers** — Develop and test intelligent radio applications for embedded and edge computing environments.
